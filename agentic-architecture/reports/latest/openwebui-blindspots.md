## Blind-spot hypotheses for review

- The OpenWebUI surface should be checked against the full capability/service inventory, not only against chat-facing adapters.
- The agentic core needs an explicit domain/process model: Case, Actor/Human, Capability, Evidence Requirement, Process State, Tool Contract.
- Services without route/capability/manifest references are likely invisible to generic chat/tool routing even if they exist technically.
- Energy-domain clusters such as Redispatch, Forecast, EDM/MaKo, Grid Connection, Gas/Wärmeplanung, VNB assessment, HITL, and Tenant provisioning should be mapped to capabilities instead of filename keywords.
